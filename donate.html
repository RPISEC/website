How to Donate

We greatly appreciate any and all donations to help RPISEC continue its mission of fostering computer security education and providing resources to our members.

  1. Go to the RPI Giving Form.
  2. Under "Designations" select Other, and enter: "RPISEC Excellence Fund (640784)" as the designation.
  3. Continue the donation form as normal.
